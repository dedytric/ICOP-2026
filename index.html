Background & Context

Opportunity

Digital waqf platforms increase accessibility, transparency, and fundraising reach for Islamic social finance.

Threat

Risks of transaction fraud (fake donations, misappropriation) directly reduce donor trust & long-term sustainability.

"Fraud not only causes financial loss but also destroys the spiritual confidence of waqf donors."

→ Urgent need for real-time, accurate detection system
🔍

Research Gap & Problem Statement

Existing fraud detection in waqf is either:

  • ✖ Manual & rule-based → Low adaptability to new fraud patterns
  • ✖ Designed for banking/fintech → Not optimized for waqf transaction characteristics
  • ✖ Ignoring extreme class imbalance (fraud cases << normal)

🚩 This research addresses:

ML-based fraud detection tailored for digital waqf with extreme imbalance and hybrid governance.

⚙️

Methodology: CRISP-DM Pipeline

Business Understanding Data Prep Modeling Evaluation

Dataset: 284,807 rows, fraud prevalence 0.17%. Removed 1081 duplicates → 283,726 rows. Stratified 80:20 split.

Models: Random Forest, XGBoost (supervised) — Isolation Forest, Autoencoder (unsupervised).

Threshold calibration: RF threshold 0.527 (precision-priority), XGBoost 0.415 (recall-priority), IF & AE use 99th percentile anomaly score.

Validation strategy:

Repeated Stratified Cross-Validation (5 folds × 3 seeds = 15 runs per model)

Metrics: Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC + pairwise Welch t-test.

Class‑weighting used for supervised models (rare class weight ~300).

✨

Conclusion & Digital Waqf Roadmap

📌 ML-based fraud detection is viable & effective for digital waqf – achieving 97.5% AUC despite extreme imbalance.

🏆 Key takeaways

• XGBoost (best overall) and Random Forest (highest precision) are statistically tied — choose based on institutional risk appetite.

• Layered governance (supervised + anomaly + human) is the recommended pattern.

🔬 Future work

• Real waqf transaction data, explainable AI (XAI) for shariah audit, and federated learning across waqf platforms.
